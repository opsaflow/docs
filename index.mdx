## Why Rivestack

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Vector search built-in" icon="brain">
    pgvector and pgvectorscale are enabled by default. Build RAG, semantic search, and recommendation systems with HNSW or StreamingDiskANN indexes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automatic failover" icon="shield-halved">
    Multi-node clusters use Patroni-managed streaming replication and automatic primary routing. Applications should reconnect during failover, and asynchronous replication can lose recent unreplicated transactions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Daily backups" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Automated daily backups with 7-day Solo or 14-day HA retention, plus point-in-time recovery within the retained WAL window.
  </Card>
</Columns>
